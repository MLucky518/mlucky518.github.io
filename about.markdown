      <h2>My Story</h2>
      <p>I've had an on and off relationship with hope throughout my life. Growing up wasn't easy. Poverty, hardship, and a lot of reasons to give up. But I was always a smart kid, and that gave me hope for a future that was different.</p>
      
      <p>As I got older, that hope got harder to hold onto. I spent years feeling undervalued, working jobs that didn't fit, carrying the weight of a past that made people see me differently. I had two kids and the last thing I wanted was to give them the life I had growing up.</p>
      
      <p>Then I discovered programming. I'd always loved computers, and I started teaching myself to code. Sleepless nights, frustration, self doubt, but I kept going. I eventually enrolled in a coding bootcamp and finished the program while recovering from surgery, never quitting even when hope felt far away.</p>
      
      <p>That hard work got me my start in tech. And I'm still going. Still learning. Still seeking knowledge every single day. Because the more I learn, the more I realize how much more there is to discover.</p>
