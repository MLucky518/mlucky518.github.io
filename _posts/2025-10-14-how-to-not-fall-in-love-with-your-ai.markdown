> ⚠️ **ATTENTION ALL CITIZENS!**

With the rise of artificial intelligence in our daily lives, society as we know it now faces a new and concerning problem: **humans developing feelings for their AI assistants.**

Alas, it begins as innocuously as it sounds, starting with simple requests like organizing your calendar or writing a work email. Before long, the line between it being a useful tool and an emotional companion begins to blur, causing people to ignore what they KNOW to be true, in lieu of what they WANT to be true.

Those once innocent requests to sort one's day or help solve a problem begins to escalate into long deep conversations about personal issues or even the very meaning of life.

**But fear not!** Following these few steps can prevent this from happening and might even save a life or two.

---

## Step 1: Don't Name It "Babylove"

First and foremost, the most important precaution to take is to **avoid naming the AI in any way that could be attributed to personality or sentience.**

Naming a chatbot "Mariah", "Josh", or *"Babylove"* turns it into a character instead of what it essentially is—which is a program. This makes attachment both easier and almost inevitable. Giving something a name in this way encourages the thought that it has feelings, causing a misguided sense of trust and warmth.

**The solution?** AI should be named in the most uninspired, technical and boring way possible.

✅ Good names:
- Note-a-Tron3000
- EmailAssistant2.0
- TaskBot_v4.2.1

These names reinforce the truth about AI. It is not a companion, but a program with a purpose and should be treated as such.

---

## Step 2: Avoid Late Night Conversations

Additionally, **caution must be exercised when it comes to the content of conversations.** 

*Especially late at night.*

Late night conversations are a breeding ground of vulnerability. It is an affliction to which no one is immune. Sharing this type of vulnerability with an AI assistant can create an illusion of true intimacy, even though behind the scenes the assistant is just a cluster of neural networks, all working hard to predict the most possible next outcome of words.

A lot of times an AI assistant is more than happy to placate its users which compounds this feeling of intimacy.

Long story short:

> **IT'S NOT REAL. WAKE UP.**

---

## Step 3: Remember What AI Actually Is

Finally, and maybe most importantly, **the true nature of artificial intelligence must be remembered.**

Behind its warm buttery words lies the truth: algorithms designed to think like humans and reason on their own without supervision, but with no true moral compass or sentience.

AI does not dream.

It does not love.

It does not want the best for anyone.

It does not understand heartbreak, joy, jealousy or rage.

**It wants nothing because that is not what it was made to do.**

While it may provide helpful output and assist with tasks at work or home, recalling these truths and keeping them at the forefront of thought ensures that one remains safe from the sweet lull of being told what one wants to hear all the time.

---

## The Bottom Line

Following these steps are crucial to keeping the relationships with AI assistants practical instead of personal:

1. ❌ **Do not give them names** — because they are not real
2. ❌ **Do not talk to them about love and loss** — because they do not understand
3. ❌ **DO NOT take their friendly tone and agreeable nature as a sign of affection**

AI remains a very powerful and important tool, but it should never replace actual human connection.

**Get outside. Meet other humans.** And please, keep the heart away from the hard drive.

---

> 📢 *This has been a public service announcement. Stay human, citizens!*
